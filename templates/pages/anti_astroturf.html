Our Anti-Astroturf Policy

We are committed to ensuring discussions reflect authentic voices. Astroturfing—coordinated or paid campaigns masquerading as grassroots participation—is not welcome on SureJan.

  • No undisclosed paid promotions or campaigns.
  • No vote manipulation or brigading.
  • Organizations and officials must identify themselves.
