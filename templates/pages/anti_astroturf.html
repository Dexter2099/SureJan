Anti-Astroturf Policy

Our anti-astroturf system, AstroShield v1, continuously scores posts and accounts based on activity signals. The score ranges from 1–100 and helps moderators detect and slow down coordinated manipulation.

Signals we monitor

  • Account age: New accounts (<3 days) add risk.
  • Vote velocity: Unusual spikes in votes per minute raise scores.
  • Posting activity: Excessive posts per day increase risk.
  • Discussion ratio: Accounts with very low comment-to-upvote ratios may be flagged.
  • Domain repetition: Heavy link posting to the same domain triggers additional scrutiny.

Score bands and actions

  • 0–39 (Normal): No action.
  • 40–69 (Watch): Amber chip appears.
  • 70–84 (High risk): Red chip appears, and slowmode (60s per user) is enforced.
  • 85+ (Severe risk): Extended slowmode (120s per user), flagged to moderators.

Scores decay naturally as activity returns to normal. All signals are based on aggregate patterns, not individual identities.

Privacy Assurances

We never sell or share personal data. AstroShield reviews patterns of activity only, not private user information.
