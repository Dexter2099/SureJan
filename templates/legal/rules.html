📖 Community Rules (SureJan)

SureJan is about open, respectful discussion. These are the ground rules:

  • Be Free, But Respectful – Debate is welcome; harassment is not.
  • No Spam or Scams – Keep the feed clear of junk, ads, or malicious links.
  • Legal Content Only – No illegal material, threats, or incitement.
  • Mark Sensitive Content – If posting NSFW or graphic content, clearly label it.
  • Local First – Since SureJan is about Brisbane, local topics are encouraged.
  • Minimal Moderation – We prefer self-regulation. Mods will only step in if things go off the rails.
