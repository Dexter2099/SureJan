SureJan. Brisbane’s no-nonsense forum

Our mission is to give Brisbane locals a simple, independent place to talk with each other, free from big-tech algorithms, surveillance, and astroturfing. We’re building an old-school, fast forum where real people, not manufactured campaigns, set the tone.

What we stand for

  • Local-first: Built for and by Brisbane. Community needs beat growth hacks.
  • Authenticity over astroturf: Guardrails against coordinated manipulation; clear rules and visible moderation.
  • Privacy by default: Minimal data collection, no tracking, and plain username/password accounts.
  • Simplicity & speed: Old-reddit style, readable on any device, no bloat.
  • Transparency & fairness: Public guidelines, community-led moderation, and decisions explained.
  • Respectful debate: Disagree well, no harassment, no brigading.

What we won’t do

  • No algorithmic engagement traps.
  • No shadowy pay-to-play or undisclosed promotions.
  • No selling your data.

Why “SureJan”?

Because we’ve all seen manufactured “grassroots” takes. This space is for Brisbane’s real voices—clear, human, and accountable.

What is “Astroturfing”?

Astroturfing is when coordinated groups or paid operators pretend to be ordinary users to manufacture the appearance of “grass-roots” opinion—seeding posts, comments, and votes to sway what looks popular or true.

Why it thrives on Reddit

  • Low cost, high reach: Many pseudonymous accounts can reach huge audiences.
  • Vote-driven ranking: Early, coordinated upvotes can game “Hot/Best”.
  • Asymmetric moderation: Gaps across thousands of subreddits.
  • Automation & marketplaces: Cheap scripts and paid “engagement”.
  • Weak identity signals: Hard to distinguish genuine locals from brigades.
