SureJan, Brisbane’s no-nonsense forum

Mission: Give Brisbane locals a simple, independent place to talk with each other, free from big-tech algorithms, surveillance, and astroturfing. We’re building an old-school, fast forum where real people, not manufactured campaigns, set the tone.

What we stand for

  • Local-first: Built for and by Brisbane, community needs beat growth hacks.
  • Authenticity over astroturf: Guardrails against coordinated manipulation, clear rules and visible moderation.
  • Privacy by default: Minimal data collection, no tracking, plain username and password accounts.
  • Simplicity & speed: Old-reddit style, readable on any device, no bloat.
  • Transparency & fairness: Public guidelines, community-led moderation, decisions explained.
  • Respectful debate: Disagree well, no harassment, no brigading.

What we won’t do

  • No algorithmic engagement traps.
  • No shadowy pay-to-play or undisclosed promotions.
  • No selling your data.

Why “SureJan”?

Because we’ve all seen manufactured “grassroots” takes, this space is for Brisbane’s real voices, clear, human, and accountable.

What is “Astroturfing”?

Astroturfing is when coordinated groups or paid operators pretend to be ordinary users to manufacture the appearance of “grass-roots” opinion, seeding posts, comments, and votes to sway what looks popular or true.

Why it thrives on Reddit

  • Low cost, high reach: One person, or a botnet, can spin up many pseudonymous accounts and reach huge audiences.
  • Vote-driven ranking: The “Hot/Best” algorithms reward early, coordinated upvotes, easy to game with brigades or sockpuppets.
  • Asymmetric moderation: Thousands of subreddits with uneven rules and volunteer mods create gaps to exploit.
  • Automation & marketplaces: Cheap scripts and paid engagement markets make mass posting and upvoting trivial.
  • Weak identity signals: Minimal verification makes it hard to distinguish genuine locals from coordinated campaigns.
