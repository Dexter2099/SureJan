Astroturf detection methods

We monitor voting patterns to spot coordinated campaigns while protecting individual privacy.

Time windows

Votes are analysed in {{ ASTRO_WINDOW_S }}‑second windows and grouped into {{ ASTRO_BUCKET_S }}‑second buckets. Baseline activity comes from the last {{ ASTRO_BASELINE_LOOKBACK_D }} days.

Definitions

  • New accounts: accounts less than {{ ASTRO_NEW_ACCOUNT_DAYS }} days old.
  • Early votes: the first {{ ASTRO_EARLY_VOTES_N }} votes on a post.

Thresholds

A post is flagged when at least {{ ASTRO_MIN_EARLY_VOTES }} early votes are cast and {{ ASTRO_EARLY_SHARE_RED_PCT }}% or more come from new accounts.

Privacy assurances

Only aggregate patterns are reviewed. Individual identities and private data are never sold or shared.

Example

If a post receives {{ ASTRO_EARLY_VOTES_N }} early votes and {{ ASTRO_EARLY_SHARE_RED_PCT }}% are from accounts younger than {{ ASTRO_NEW_ACCOUNT_DAYS }} days, moderators take a closer look.
